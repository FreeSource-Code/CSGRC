Publication Scope & Vision

About The Book & Scholarly Objectives

Addressing the critical convergence between artificial intelligence, emerging multi-jurisdictional compliance mandates, and resilient technical cybersecurity operations.

Executive Context & Background

The rapid adoption of Artificial Intelligence (AI), cloud computing, and digital transformation has fundamentally reshaped the cybersecurity landscape.

Organizations across industries face increasingly sophisticated cyber threats, evolving regulatory obligations, and growing demands for effective governance, risk management, and compliance (GRC). Traditional cybersecurity approaches are no longer sufficient to address the challenges presented by AI-enabled systems, interconnected digital ecosystems, and constantly changing global regulations.

Consequently, organizations require integrated frameworks that combine advanced technologies with strategic governance and regulatory compliance to ensure cyber resilience and sustainable business operations.

Core Scholarly Objectives

  • • Bridging Technical & Boardroom GRC: Connecting deep technical zero-trust controls, telemetry, and vulnerability management with executive board-level oversight and fiduciary duties.
  • • Navigating Global Regulatory Matrix: Deconstructing the implications of statutory frameworks including the European Union AI Act, GDPR, NIS2, DORA, and US SEC cyber disclosure mandates.
  • • Harnessing AI Safely: Evaluating dual-use artificial intelligence architectures—leveraging predictive analytics for automated risk mitigation while securing models against adversarial poisoning and hallucination.
  • • Continuous Cyber Resilience: Moving beyond check-box compliance audits to automated, continuous compliance monitoring (CCM) and incident orchestration.
