Technology moves fast.
Governance must keep pace.

The rapid adoption of Artificial Intelligence, cloud computing, and digital transformation has fundamentally reshaped the cybersecurity landscape. Organizations face increasingly sophisticated cyber threats, evolving regulatory obligations, and growing demands for effective governance, risk management, and compliance.

Beyond traditional security

AI-enabled systems and interconnected digital ecosystems require new approaches to constantly changing global regulations.

From compliance to resilience

This book explores integrated frameworks that combine advanced technologies, strategic governance, and regulatory compliance to ensure cyber resilience and sustainable business operations.
