ABOUT
THE BOOK

Publisher: Bentham Science
Indexing: Scopus Indexed
Publication: Volume 2026

The rapid adoption of Artificial Intelligence (AI), cloud computing, and digital transformation has fundamentally reshaped the cybersecurity landscape. Organizations across industries face increasingly sophisticated cyber threats, evolving regulatory obligations, and growing demands for effective governance, risk management, and compliance (GRC).

Traditional cybersecurity approaches are no longer sufficient to address the challenges presented by AI-enabled systems, interconnected digital ecosystems, and constantly changing global regulations. Consequently, organizations require integrated frameworks that combine advanced technologies with strategic governance and regulatory compliance to ensure cyber resilience and sustainable business operations.
